The reporting question

How can a commercial team move from disconnected sales data to a consistent view of revenue, profit, product performance, regional contribution and delivery choices?

This project develops a 2024 performance dashboard for Infinity Exports, consolidating transactional, customer, product and logistics information into a decision-oriented Power BI report.

What the dashboard covers

  • Commercial performance: revenue, total profit, product margin and trends through the year.
  • Product decisions: revenue and volume by category to distinguish high-volume from higher-value products.
  • Customers & markets: customer contribution and country-level concentration.
  • Operational trade-offs: shipping mode, business channel and the risk of changing logistics strategies.

Representative insights

The analysed dataset contains £1.04B in recorded sales and a 44.92% average profit margin as reported in the public project README. These are dataset measures, not claimed gains from the dashboard.

Apparel categories such as jackets, cardigans and hoodies contributed strongly to revenue in the analysis. The reporting also highlighted questions about regional concentration, category profitability and the trade-off between cheaper delivery modes and customer experience.

A useful dashboard should reveal what decision needs attention — not simply show more charts.

How it supports decisions

  1. Compare margin and volume before promoting product categories.
  2. Investigate country or account concentration when planning commercial investment.
  3. Explore sales trends by month and week before forecasting inventory or campaigns.
  4. Use delivery-mode analysis to frame logistics changes as cost, revenue and service trade-offs.

The project provides decision support, rather than evidence that these operational recommendations were implemented.

Evidence

The public GitHub repository contains the Power BI file, supporting datasets, dashboard screenshots and explanatory walkthroughs.
