The customer problem

Search is one of the most direct expressions of shopping intent. When queries fail, return irrelevant products or handle attributes poorly, customers have fewer chances to reach a product that meets their needs.

The engagement focused on improving how product search handled both structured attributes and more natural-language intent.

Analytical work

  1. Diagnosed failed searches: examined search outputs and recurring query patterns to identify avoidable friction.
  2. Defined a hybrid approach: combined structured SQL-based filtering with semantic vector retrieval to balance exact product constraints and flexible intent matching.
  3. Validated improvements: considered relevance, failed-query rates and downstream customer experience signals.
  4. Communicated business impact: translated search performance into conversion and support implications.
