The business problem

CloudFlow is a subscription-business analysis project focused on recurring revenue, retention and customer experience. The central questions were where revenue was growing or fluctuating, why customers were leaving and where the upgrade/payment journey introduced friction.

The analysis uses nine related tables covering subscriptions, customers, products, usage, payments and cancellations.

Analytical approach

  1. Structured the business questions around revenue, retention, conversion and expansion.
  2. Explored plan-level monthly revenue and variability across 2022–2023 using SQL.
  3. Examined usage behaviour, support interactions and payment-funnel completion.
  4. Analysed cancellation reasons and identified customer accounts worth investigating for upsell.
  5. Translated the patterns into specific commercial hypotheses and potential interventions.

Premium subscriptions contributed the highest average monthly revenue among the plans examined, while lower-tier revenue showed pronounced volatility. Cancellation analysis also revealed price sensitivity as a meaningful reason for churn.

Beyond retention, SQL-based account analysis highlighted 122 potential upsell accounts for closer commercial review. These represent opportunities in the project dataset, not realised sales.

Recommendations

  • Reduce payment friction: examine errors and simplify steps that cause customers to abandon checkout.
  • Prioritise account expansion: qualify single-product accounts with potential to adopt additional modules.
  • Address churn drivers: test pricing/value communication and retention interventions for customers showing price sensitivity.

These are analytical recommendations; the published repository does not demonstrate that a business deployed them or achieved the projected results.

Evidence

The repository includes SQL scripts, datasets, schema documentation, analysis outputs and supporting Power BI graphics.
